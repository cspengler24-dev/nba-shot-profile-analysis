## Overview

Analysis of NBA shot attempt data from the 2023-24 season using R and the tidyverse.

Dataset size: 218,701 shot attempts.

## Tools

- R
- tidyverse
- dplyr
- ggplot2

## Visualizations

### Top Shot Attempt Leaders
Identifies the players with the highest shot volume.

### Shot Profile Comparison
Compares shot distributions across distance ranges for selected players.

### Shot Profile Percentages
Shows the percentage of total shots taken from each distance range.

### Efficiency vs Volume
Examines the relationship between field goal percentage and shot attempts.

### Most Efficient High-Volume Scorers
Ranks players with at least 500 shot attempts by field goal percentage.

## Key Findings

- Stephen Curry took most of his shots from three-point range.
- Giannis Antetokounmpo generated most of his attempts near the basket.
- High shot volume does not guarantee higher shooting efficiency.
- Interior players generally had the highest field goal percentages.
